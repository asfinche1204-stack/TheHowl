The number
1,662.54

Total points scored in the league on Sunday, 107 more than any other week this season and 234 more than Week 3. Nine of the twelve teams cleared 130.

Handy Cramp lost by 0.42 points, and to get there it had to break its own record. At Sunday night's read Andy sat on 84.04 with three starters still to play, 60.22 behind a She Gibbs it up until i Corum side that had already finished on 144.26. Puka Nacua went 29.2, Jared Goff 28.94, and the number climbed to 143.84 — a post-read swing of 59.80, two hundredths of a point bigger than the +59.78 he set seven days earlier, which was itself the biggest this paper had recorded. He has now made up sixty points after press time in consecutive weeks. The first one won him the game by 24.92. The second one lost it by 0.42, which is the closest this league has ever been.

Forsaken Dave won a game. Dave McQ. was 30.52 behind Poop Poop with three starters out at the read and finished on 157.98 — his best of the season — with Jacoby Brissett, a free agent he picked up at seven o'clock on Wednesday morning, leading him on 24.78. He is 1-4 with 634.16 points scored, which is the fourth-largest total in the league, and 703.54 against. The team has also now been renamed twice in six days: Righteous Dave became Foresaken Dave at 6:52 on Tuesday morning, and Foresaken Dave became Forsaken Dave at 10:22 on Sunday night. The second one was a spelling correction.

This paper's own team posted 184.00, the second-biggest number anybody has managed this season, after two consecutive weeks in the nineties. Josh Allen's 30.5 arrived on Sunday night and took the whole thing from 128.00 to where it finished. Two Chinz got 112.60 and 14.2 from Jacory Croskey-Merritt, a running back Pedantic dropped on Wednesday night to make room for Aaron Rodgers and Matthew claimed off waivers on Friday morning. Rodgers went 22.0. Both teams were right.

The trade settled itself. Nine days after the Waiver Wire Wizards sent George Pickens to the Raleighwood Boo Bears for DJ Moore and Josh Downs, Pickens put 23.5 into a 174.44 that beat Brad by 37.40, with Moore and Downs both on the Wizards' bench. Brad's 137.04 was the third-best score of the week and would have beaten nine of the other eleven teams in the league; it went 9-2 against the field and 0-1 where it counted. The Boo Bears now have 711.24 points, the most in the league, taken off Pedantic on Sunday.

Pedantic is 5-0 anyway, and is a 7.3-point underdog on Sunday. The unbeaten team projects 108.5 against Two Chinz's 115.8 with nine starters active and Lamar Jackson questionable on his bench. The highest projection on the board — 125.5, the only full eleven-man lineup in the league — belongs to The Real Chargers, who are 0-5 with 726.08 points against and have finally moved Patrick Mahomes into the starting quarterback slot. This column spent two weeks pointing at that bench. It is a strange week to be writing both of those sentences.

The league scored 1,662.54 points on Sunday, 107 more than in any other week this season. Nine of the twelve teams went past 130. Six teams are now within a game of each other at 3-2 or 2-3, the top two are separated by half a game, and the bottom two have between them one win and 1,429.62 points against.
