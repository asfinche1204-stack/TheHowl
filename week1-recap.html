The bag
Gibbs team, +19.4

Biggest beat of the Sunday-night projection: 125.4 projected, 144.80 final. Handy Cramp went the other way, 123.4 projected, 106.14 final, and won anyway.

She Gibbs it up until i Corum was down 26.76 at the read with three players left. Jaxson Dart went for 26.6 and Javonte Williams 21.7 in the Cowboys–Giants game, the Monday player added the rest, and the deficit became a 21.68 win, 144.80 to 123.12. Two Chinz's one Sunday-night player added exactly nothing. That is the biggest post-read swing in the league, +48.44, and the first entry in the record book that nobody wanted their name on.

The Real Chargers scored 151.46, the third-best number in the league, would have beaten nine of the other eleven teams, and lost, because Spreadin' dem Sheets scored 158.26. Josh Allen 35.66 and D'Andre Swift 31.9 held; the Chargers got 30.9 after the read, Isaiah Likely 23.8 leading it, and came up 6.8 short. The luck index has the Chargers at −0.82, the worst in the league, and the power rankings have them sixth at 0-1, which is the rating doing its job.

Handy Cramp needed 10.3 from three players against a finished Poop Poop and got 12.04. 106.14 to 104.36, the closest game of the week and the lowest score to win one. Handy Cramp was 2-9 against the field. The 0.94 game didn't stay one: The Raleighwood Boo Bears got 32.6 from Kenneth Walker III on Monday night and won 148.20 to 114.46, while Righteous Dave's two Monday players added 6.2. Caleb Williams's 37.26 is still the best individual line of the week, and it still lost by 33. PedanticGaspOfaFart's 174.56 stood as the high score — nobody came within 16 — and The People's Champ finished at 98.02, 0-11 against the field. Waiver Wire Wizards over the Raleighwood Chargers, 121.20 to 110.04, was the only game that ended Sunday, and it ended the way it looked.

The wire since the draft: Spreadin' dem Sheets has spent $104 of the $121 the whole league has put into FAAB — $82 on MarShawn Lloyd, $22 on Tyler Allgeier — and the two receivers it cut to make room, Xavier Worthy and Brian Thomas Jr., were picked up by PedanticGaspOfaFart and the Boo Bears. The Real Chargers paid $15 for Patrick Mahomes off waivers and added a linebacker at 8:13 Sunday morning. The Gibbs team leads the league with five adds, including dropping Jake Bates for Jonathan Greenard on a Friday and re-signing Bates on the Saturday. Four teams — Wizards, Raleighwood Chargers, Righteous Dave, People's Champ — have not made a move since the draft, and three of them are 0-1.
