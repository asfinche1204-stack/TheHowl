PedanticGaspOfaFart is 6-0, and it got there by 1.06 points with the worst score it has posted all season. The Waiver Wire Wizards scored 74.42, which is the lowest number anybody has posted in this league. It beats The Real Chargers' 86.94 from Week 3 by more than twelve points, and it came three hours after Brad completed his second trade of the season — Cam Skattebo and DeVonta Smith out, Luther Burden III and Kyle Monangai in. This paper's team has the league points lead. 158.90 was the best score of the week — Nabers 24.0, Swift 21.0, Golden 20.9, and eight more contributions behind them — and it took 849.72 past the Boo Bears' 840.08, three hours after Ian traded two starters away. Ian had the two best individual lines in the league on Sunday, Trevor Lawrence at 27.34 and Kenneth Walker at 26.9, and lost by 30.06. He still has the best all-play record in the league at 48-18.

The Real Chargers won a game. Collin was 0-5 with 594.34 points against through four weeks, and this column spent three consecutive Tuesdays pointing at Patrick Mahomes on his bench. Mahomes has started the last two weeks, the lineup has been complete both times, and on Sunday the team put 121.58 on the Wizards and won by 47.16 — exactly the kind of result our book predicted when it made the 0-5 side a 30.3-point favourite.

Forsaken Dave is the other story and it is a worse one. 109.94 and beaten by 30.46, which makes 1-5 on a 36-30 all-play record. The luck index reads −2.27, the worst figure this paper has printed in either league this season, and the 843.94 points scored against him are a league record.
