The bag
Righteous Dave, +23.8

Biggest beat of the Sunday-night projection: 106.8 projected, 130.62 final. The Gibbs team went the other way, 136.0 projected, 117.50 final, −18.5.

Spreadin' dem Sheets scored 156.62, the high score of the week by 23, and did most of it with two players: Josh Allen's 40.82 is the best individual line of the season and Jaxon Smith-Njigba's 38.0 is second, which is 78.82 out of a lineup of eleven. It also drew the week's third-best score. Righteous Dave put up 130.62, went 9-2 against the field, got Davante Adams's 37.0 on Monday night, and lost by 26. Dave is 0-2 with the most points against in the league, 304.82, and he has made five moves — more than anybody — to get there.

The Boo Bears–Gibbs game did not stay at 0.76. Kenneth Walker III's 20.8 and Tyler Warren on Sunday night, then Cam Skattebo on Monday, turned a one-point deficit into a 125.64–117.50 win and moved Ian to 2-0. Devin Lloyd scored 31.0 from linebacker for the losing side, the fifth-best line in the league this week, and Jalen Coker — the $101 waiver claim, now the most expensive player in the league by nineteen dollars — scored 10.6 in a game his team lost by 8.14.

PedanticGaspOfaFart finished at 111.10 on Sunday and won by 2.52. The Real Chargers got Jonathan Taylor from 14.4 up to 27.2 and had Isaiah Likely on Monday, and the projection that said 3.9 was almost exactly right. That makes them 0-2 by a combined 8.82 points with the fifth-most points in the league, which the luck index scores at −1.18, tied for the worst. The Waiver Wire Wizards, meanwhile, are 2-0 on an 8-14 record against the field: Harrison Mevis, the Monday-night kicker this paper said would decide their game, scored nothing, and they won by 2.98 anyway. Poop Poop is 0-2 by a combined 3.76 points across two weeks, which may be the most anyone has lost by while losing this little.

Three housekeeping notes. ESPN restated three Week 1 scores between last Tuesday and Sunday — the Real Chargers up half a point, Righteous Dave up 1.5, Handy Cramp down one — which moves the closest game of the season from 1.78 to 0.78 and the best score in a loss to 151.96; the corrections table above has all of it. ESPN's playoff odds add up to 600%, so six of twelve make it, and the odds machine is set accordingly. And the book went 5-1 straight up but only 3-3 against its own spreads, which is about right for a week when two games were decided by fewer than three points.
